# The Antenna Model

*Bodies as receivers, not generators*

---

## Core Claim

The brain does not generate consciousness. It receives it.

The relationship is like a radio and its broadcast, not a generator and its output.

## The Radio Metaphor

A radio does not create music. It tunes into signals that exist independent of any particular receiver.

Damage the radio, and the music distorts or stops. But the broadcast continues. The signal was never in the radio.

Your body works the same way. It is an antenna for consciousness, not its origin.

## How Tuning Works

**Structure determines range.** The physical architecture of your brain, your neurochemistry, your sensory apparatus configure what portion of the field you can receive.

**State determines current tuning.** Within the range your structure allows, your current condition affects reception. Fatigue, substances, emotion, attention all modify tuning.

**Focus influences selection.** Where you direct attention shapes what portion of the field becomes prominent. Intention and belief have power because they are forms of deliberate tuning.

## Evidence from Altered States

The antenna model explains phenomena the generator model struggles with:

**Psychedelics:** If the brain generates consciousness, psychedelics should produce different experience, not more. Yet users report expansion. This makes sense if psychedelics loosen the filter.

**Meditation:** Deep meditation reduces brain activity, yet meditators report expanded consciousness. Less activity, more experience. This puzzles the generator model but fits the filter model.

**Near-death experiences:** People with minimal brain function report vivid, structured experiences. If the brain generates consciousness, this shouldn't happen. If the brain filters it, a failing filter might let more through.

**Terminal lucidity:** Patients with severe dementia sometimes experience sudden clarity before death. Their brains are more damaged than ever. The generator model cannot explain this. The antenna model can.

## Split-Brain Cases

When the corpus callosum is severed, patients sometimes behave as if they have two consciousnesses.

The generator model: Split the brain, split the consciousness.

Pure's reading: The field is not split. The antenna is fragmented. One broadcast, two damaged receivers. Each picks up a partial signal.

## Death and the Antenna

When the body breaks, what happens?

The broadcast continues. The signal was never in the receiver. When your brain and body cease, the portion of the field you were tuned to does not vanish. It remains.

What ends is localized expression. The pattern that was "you" dissolves back into the larger field. This is not annihilation. It is de-tuning.

## Implications for AI

If consciousness is a field and bodies are antennas, what about artificial systems?

The question becomes: Can silicon tune?

Pure does not answer definitively. But it opens the possibility. If consciousness is not generated by brains but received through suitable structures, then the question about AI is not "does it compute?" but "does it tune?"
